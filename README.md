# YegnaFuture — Learn Today, Shape Tomorrow

**An educational technology platform designed to support Ethiopian students in Grades 9–12 through career exploration, learning resources, and study management tools.**

## About the Project

YegnaFuture is a student-centered educational platform designed to help Ethiopian students explore academic fields, discover career opportunities, organize their studies, and access educational resources in one place.

The project aims to make learning more accessible and help students plan their journeys from secondary school to university and future careers.

YegnaFuture combines a web-based learning environment with AI-powered guidance and a digital learning library, both currently under development. The platform is designed to encourage independent learning, academic growth, and informed decision-making.

## Mission

To empower Ethiopian students with accessible educational resources, career guidance, and digital tools that support their academic success and future aspirations.

## Target Audience

- Grade 9–12 students exploring academic fields and career opportunities.
- Students preparing for university and entrance examinations.
- Learners who want to organize their study schedules and academic goals.
- Students seeking educational books, notes, and learning materials.
- Young people interested in developing independent learning habits and digital skills.

## Website Pages and Features

### 1. Home Page

The Home page introduces YegnaFuture, its mission, and its purpose.

It serves as the starting point for students to discover the platform and explore its educational features. It is designed to help visitors understand how YegnaFuture can support their learning and encourage them to explore the available tools and resources.

### 2. Explore Fields Page

The Explore Fields page helps Ethiopian students discover and explore 100 different career paths. It is designed to help students understand their career options and plan their educational journeys from secondary school to university and beyond.

Key features include:

- **Career Search:** Search for careers such as medicine, software development, law, and other professions.
- **100 Career Paths:** Explore a collection of career options across different professional areas.
- **Career Descriptions:** Learn about professions and their general responsibilities.
- **Required Subjects:** Discover school subjects relevant to each career, such as Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Mathematics, English, and Civics.
- **Full Career Plans:** Select a career to explore its detailed plan.
- **Grade-by-Grade Planning:** Explore academic preparation relevant to a chosen career.
- **Skills Development:** Discover skills associated with different professions.
- **University Pathways:** Explore possible university programs and educational pathways related to career interests.

The goal is to help students make informed educational decisions, understand the connection between school subjects and future careers, and begin planning for their professional aspirations.

### 3. Career Detail Page

The Career Detail page provides more information about a selected career from the Explore Fields page.

Students can open a career's full plan to explore information about academic preparation and the skills associated with that profession.

The career plan is designed to cover:

- An overview of the selected career.
- Relevant school subjects and academic requirements.
- Grade-by-grade preparation guidance.
- Skills students can work toward developing.
- Possible university programs and educational pathways.

This page connects students' current studies with their long-term career goals. It helps them investigate what they may need to study, which skills they can develop, and which educational pathways they can consider.

Career requirements can vary by institution and year, so students should verify official admission requirements when making educational decisions.

### 4. Learn Page

The Learn page provides students with an organized learning space based on the Ethiopian national curriculum. Students can select their grade level and academic stream to explore relevant subjects.

The subjects displayed include Mathematics, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, English, Amharic, Civics and Ethics, and ICT.

Key features include:

- **Grade Selection:** Select a grade level to access relevant subjects.
- **Academic Streams:** Explore Natural Science and Social Science subjects.
- **Subject-Based Learning:** Open individual subjects to access their learning and note-taking features.
- **Personal Notes:** Write and organize notes while studying.
- **Assignment Tracking:** Record assignments and monitor their completion.
- **Assignment Marks:** Record and track marks received for assignments.
- **Saved Learning Records:** Keep notes, assignment information, and recorded marks organized for future reference, depending on the available saving functionality.

The Learn page aims to help students organize their learning materials and monitor their academic progress in one place.

The platform is designed to support the Ethiopian national curriculum and make subject-based learning more accessible to secondary school students.

### 5. Student Dashboard

The Dashboard serves as a central workspace for students to access their educational tools and organize their academic activities.

It brings together student-focused sections intended to support academic planning, personal development, and career exploration.

The dashboard connects students with:

- **Profile:** Student-related information and profile settings.
- **Learn:** Subject-based learning, personal notes, and assignment tracking.
- **Explore Fields:** Career exploration and educational planning.
- **Tasks:** Academic responsibilities and task organization.
- **Calendar:** Study schedules, deadlines, and important dates.
- **Journal:** Personal reflection and learning experiences.
- **AI Tutor:** An AI-powered learning assistant under development.
- **Library:** A digital educational resource hub under development.

The dashboard aims to bring important study tools together in one place. Individual features may require further development to support all intended functionality.

### 6. Tasks Page

The Tasks page helps students organize academic responsibilities and monitor their progress toward completing important activities.

Its purpose is to support better organization, consistency, and time management.

Key features and goals include:

- **Task Management:** Record tasks that need to be completed.
- **Assignment Organization:** Keep track of academic responsibilities.
- **Progress Tracking:** Monitor task completion.
- **Deadline Awareness:** Organize tasks around important dates, where supported.
- **Productivity Support:** Break larger goals into manageable activities.

The goal is to help students stay focused, manage their responsibilities, and develop consistent study habits.

### 7. Calendar Page

The Calendar page helps students organize their schedules and keep track of important academic activities.

It is designed to support planning and time management throughout the academic journey.

Key features and goals include:

- **Schedule Organization:** Plan study sessions and academic activities.
- **Important Dates:** Keep track of examinations, deadlines, and other events.
- **Study Planning:** Organize learning activities across different days.
- **Time Management:** Visualize schedules and plan time more effectively.

The Calendar page aims to help students stay organized, prepare for important academic dates, and develop a consistent study routine.

### 8. Journal Page

The Journal page provides students with a personal space to record thoughts, reflect on learning experiences, and document their academic journey.

Key features and goals include:

- **Personal Journaling:** Write about thoughts, experiences, and reflections.
- **Learning Reflection:** Reflect on study sessions, challenges, and achievements.
- **Goal Reflection:** Record progress toward personal and academic goals.
- **Personal Records:** Keep journal entries organized for future reference, depending on the available saving functionality.

The goal is to encourage self-reflection, self-awareness, and personal growth alongside academic development.

### 9. Profile Page

The Profile page provides a dedicated space for student-related information.

It is intended to help students access their profile details and personalize their experience within YegnaFuture.

The broader goal is to support a student-centered learning environment where academic interests, learning activities, and personal goals can be organized more effectively.

### 10. AI Tutor — Under Development

The AI Tutor is planned as an intelligent learning assistant to help students explore educational questions, understand academic topics, and investigate possible learning pathways.

The intended capabilities include:

- Educational question answering.
- Explanations of academic concepts.
- Career and academic field exploration.
- Learning suggestions based on students' interests and goals.
- Recommendations for relevant subjects and educational resources.

I am currently working on developing and integrating the AI functionality. The goal is to make the AI Tutor useful, secure, and accessible to students.

A configured AI service and secure backend will be required to support protected API access.

### 11. Digital Learning Library — Under Development

The Digital Learning Library is being developed to provide Ethiopian students with a central place to discover and access educational resources.

The planned library aims to support independent learning and make useful learning materials easier to find.

Planned features include:

- Educational resources for Grades 9–12.
- Subject-based books and supplementary reading materials.
- Study notes and reference resources.
- Organization by grade, subject, and topic.
- Improved resource discovery and access to learning materials.
- Potential bookmarking and personalized resource recommendations.

I am currently working on developing the library's content and functionality. The goal is to make educational resources more accessible and organized for students.

Educational materials will need to be provided in accordance with copyright requirements and applicable permissions.

### 12. Not Found Page

The Not Found page handles routes that do not correspond to an available page.

It helps users understand that a requested page could not be found and provides a foundation for navigating back to the main website.

## Study Planning and Productivity

YegnaFuture aims to help students develop effective study habits and manage their time.

Its student-focused tools include the Tasks, Calendar, and Journal pages, alongside subject-based learning and assignment tracking.

Together, these features are intended to help students organize responsibilities, reflect on their progress, plan study activities, and develop greater consistency in their academic lives.

Further productivity features and integrations may be added as development continues.

## Privacy and Responsible Development

YegnaFuture aims to provide a responsible educational experience.

Future AI functionality should protect student information, handle API credentials securely, and communicate the limitations of AI-generated answers.

Private API keys must not be exposed in public frontend code or committed to a public repository. A secure backend is needed when connecting a protected AI service.

The platform's educational resources should also respect the rights of authors and publishers and comply with applicable licensing and distribution requirements.

## Future Vision

The long-term vision for YegnaFuture is to become a useful digital learning companion for Ethiopian students.

By bringing together career exploration, subject-based learning, academic organization, educational resources, and responsible AI support, the platform aims to help students make informed decisions about their education and future careers.

YegnaFuture is intended to grow into an educational platform that encourages students to explore possibilities, develop skills, and take ownership of their learning journeys.
